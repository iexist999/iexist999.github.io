pocketSandBlocks

Welcome!

pocketSandBlocks is a casual sand game featuring a satisfying falling-sand style cell simulation. As block shapes are falling from the top, your goal is to position them strategically, in such a way, so the sand they eventually crumble into, forms a clear connection between both walls.

With the 12 currently available game modes, you have plenty of fun mechanics to explore! Some of them were created specifically for challenge and difficulty seeking players. Others introduce completely new and unique mechanics, deviating far from the original experience :P

Here are a few examples of the fun gamemodes we've added!

  • classicSand! the classic experience. tiles crumble into sand upon landing. tiles fall one at a time and crumble into sand the moment they land. the sand piles up and spreads out on its own, so every tile you place changes the ground for the next one. link both walls with an unbroken line of sand to clear it and keep going.
  • classicSandPlus! a classic gamemode featuring extra modifications like lava blocks or bombs. everything from classicSand, plus extra pieces mixed into the falling tiles. lava blocks and bombs shake up the terrain, so you will have to plan around them (or put them to good use).
  • elementSoup! a classic gamemode with multiple materials like sand, water, stone, honey and more! sand is only the beginning. water, stone, honey and other materials each flow and settle in their own way, so it matters what your connection is made of, not just where it sits.
  • CHAOS! CHAOTIC! gamemode featuring fun events that happen randomly thoughout the game! random events strike while you play and you never know what is coming next. adapt on the fly, make the best of it and enjoy the mess!
  • brittleBricks! tiles are made out of brittle brick material, which stays solid, but can crumble upon collision tiles stay solid instead of crumbling into sand, but they are brittle. a hard enough collision breaks them apart, so how you drop and stack them really matters.
