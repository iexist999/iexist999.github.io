pocketSandBlocks

Welcome!

pocketSandBlocks is a casual sand game featuring a satisfying falling-sand style cell simulation. As block shapes are falling from the top, your goal is to position them strategically, in such a way, so the sand they eventually crumble into, forms a clear connection between both walls.

With the 12 currently available game modes, you have plenty of fun mechanics to explore! Some of them were created specifically for challenge and difficulty seeking players. Others introduce completely new and unique mechanics, deviating far from the original experience :P

Here are a few examples of the fun gamemodes we've added!

  • classicSand! the classic experience. tiles crumble into sand upon landing.
    casual singleplayer 6 difficulty modes

    GENERAL DESCRIPTION

    tiles fall one at a time and crumble into sand the moment they land. the sand piles up and disperses naturally. its behavior is dictated by the selected difficulty level.
    at lower difficulties, the sand spreads uniformly, forming symmetric pyramid-like structures. at higher ones, sand is replaced by other, less granular materials such as damp dirt or clay.

    as the game progresses, the tiles fall faster and faster, so you have less time to strategize your next move.

    your goal, is to make a connection between both of the horiztonal map borders using same-color sand. if you successfully do so, every sand pixel, that made the connection will be removed. you need to prevent sand from reaching the top of the map, in which case, if there is no more space for new tiles to spawn, the game is over.

    LIST OF MODIFICATORS:

    Lava Block - (Positive Modificator) - Modificator Rarity: Common
    a standard shaped tile, made entirely out of lava. it vaporizes any material it gets in contact with. depending on the selected difficulty level, one lava pixel may equal up to 10 removed sand pixels.

    Bomb - (Positive Modificator) - Modificator Rarity: Fairly Common
    a small black colored square, which explodes on contact with matter, removing nearby pixels in its effective range. depending on the selected difficulty level, the range of the explosion may vary slightly.

    Colorshifter - (Neutral Modificator) - Modificator Rarity: Uncommon
    a small rainbow colored square, which fires a circular field that expands, and modifies the color of the sand within its range. switch rules are applied randomly for each shifter, meaning one can turn the color green to red, while another one may turn the same color to yellow. depending on the selected difficulty level, the range of the field may vary. also, on lower difficulties, the color switch may be applied to entire piles of sand if only a part of them gets caught in range of the field.

    Rainbow Tile - (Negative Modificator) - Modificator Rarity: Uncommon
    a standard shaped tile, with a rainbow chrome effect. the player is unable to identify the color of the sand until its been placed. When placed, the rainbow tile loses its rainbow effect, and the randomly selected color of the sand is unveiled.

    Paintball - (Negative Modificator) - Modificator Rarity: Rare
    a gray ball, that pops open on contact, splashing rainbow paint on top of nearby sand, making it harder to distinct its original color.

    GENERAL DESCRIPTION

    tiles fall one at a time and crumble into different materials the moment they land. each color is assigned a random material at start, and its locked to that material for the rest of the game
    at higher difficulties, the pool of potential materials include more tricker behaving ones, like gel, or solids.

    as the game progresses, the tiles fall faster and faster, so you have less time to strategize your next move.

    your goal, is to make a connection between both of the horiztonal map borders using same-color materials. if you successfully do so, every pixel, that made the connection will be removed. you need to prevent pixels from reaching the top of the map, in which case, if there is no more space for new tiles to spawn, the game is over.

    LIST OF POTENTIAL MATERIALS/ELEMENTS:

    Smooth Sand - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Very Easy
    smooth sand is perfectly granular and forms uniform piles.

    Gravel - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Easy
    gravel is another granular material, but unlike some others, it doesnt disperse perfectly, and may form irregular piles. this is still a fairly granular material compared to others though

    Sand - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Easy
    sand, similarly to gravel, doesnt produce uniform piles. unlike gravel, sand can absorb water, making it heavier and more cohesive

    Damp Dirt - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Easy
    damp dirt is much more cohesive than sand or gravel. it forms much steeper and irregular piles.

    Coal - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Difficult
    coal forms extremely steep piles, and doesnt disperse well to the sides. piles of this material are usually more tall than they are wide.

    Sawdust - Material Type: Powder - Behavior Trickiness: Easy
    this material is much lighter than most other powders. heavier materials falling at high speeds may burrow under several layers of a sawdust pile. unlike most others, this material floats on water.

    Flour - Material Type: Ash - Behavior Trickiness: Mixed
    flour is very light and may be easily influenced by airflow. heavier materials falling at high speeds may burrow under several layers of a flour pile. this material also floats on top of water.

    Water - Material Type: Liquid - Behavior Trickiness: Mixed
    water spills horizontally filling tiny crevices. it can get absorbed by certain materials like sand. fluid flow may influence the movement of individual pixels within the liquid, while gravity causes water to equalize differences in surface height.

    Motor Oil - Material Type: Liquid - Behavior Trickiness: Mixed
    motor oil is less dense than water, thus it stays on top of it. 

    Honey - Material Type: Gel - Behavior Trickiness: Difficult
    honey is a very thick and viscous gel-like fluid. it disperses very slowly and forms gel-like formations. most powdery materials can stick, or get stuck inside this liquid.

    Stone - Material Type: Unstable Solid - Behavior Trickiness: Mixed
    Stone is a semi-solid material. it behaves like an extremely stackable powder unless it has connection with one of the map border, in which case, it turns completely solid.
