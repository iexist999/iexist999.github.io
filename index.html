pocketSandBlocks

Welcome!

pocketSandBlocks is a casual sand game featuring a satisfying falling-sand style cell simulation. As block shapes are falling from the top, your goal is to position them strategically, in such a way, so the sand they eventually crumble into, forms a clear connection between both walls.

With the 12 currently available game modes, you have plenty of fun mechanics to explore! Some of them were created specifically for challenge and difficulty seeking players. Others introduce completely new and unique mechanics, deviating far from the original experience :P

Here are a few examples of the fun gamemodes we've added!

  • classicSand! the classic experience. tiles crumble into sand upon landing.
  • classicSandPlus! a classic gamemode featuring extra modifications like lava blocks or bombs.
  • elementSoup! a classic gamemode with multiple materials like sand, water, stone, honey and more!
  • CHAOS! CHAOTIC! gamemode featuring fun events that happen randomly thoughout the game!
  • brittleBricks! tiles are made out of brittle brick material, which stays solid, but can crumble upon collision
