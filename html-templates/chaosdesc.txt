<p>tiles fall one at a time and crumble into sand the moment they land. the sand piles up and disperses naturally. its behavior is dictated by the selected difficulty level.</p>
<p>this gamemode features special (sometimes fun, sometimes not :P) events that trigger in random timespans thoughout the game. at lower difficulties, the events may be only slightly troublesome, and last a lot shorter, while on higher difficulties, the mentioned events get more disruptive on top of lasting more time.</p>
<p>as the game progresses, the tiles fall faster and faster, so you have less time to strategize your next move. however, this gamemode focuses less on linear speedup difficulty and more on the random events part of this experience.</p>
<p>your goal, is to make a connection between both of the horiztonal map borders using same-color sand. if you successfully do so, every sand pixel, that made the connection will be removed. you need to prevent sand from reaching the top of the map, in which case, if there is no more space for new tiles to spawn, the game is over.</p>
<p><strong>LIST OF RANDOM EVENTS:</strong></p>
<p><strong>Leafblowers</strong> - (Negative Event) - Disruption Level: Low<br />a random rectangular part of the map is outlined as the leafblower area. inside that area, a constant stream or airflow is pumped in a random non-fixed direction. this event can randomly generate either one big area, or a couple of smaller ones. each area has an arrow icon in the middle, pointing in the direction that the airflow is being blowed.</p>
<p><strong>Flood</strong> - (Negative Event) - Disruption Level: Mild<br />floods cause water to slowly fill the empty parts of the map. newly spawned tiles fall and move slower when going through water. upon placement, sand and other granular materials generaly disperse slower and take longer to settle down. fluid flow is generally not a concern with this event unless youre playing on higher difficulties which allow multiple events to trigger simultaneously.</p>
<p><strong>Solar Eclipse</strong> - (Negative Event) - Disruption Level: Low<br />the whole map turns pitch black, and the only light sources illuminating the game are random fireflies flying on and about.</p>
